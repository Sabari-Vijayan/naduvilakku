## ESP32 Pin Configuration

| Component            | Pin / Signal     |  ESP32 GPIO | Purpose                 |
| -------------------- | ---------------- | ----------: | ----------------------- |
| **LDR**              | AO (Analog Out)  | **GPIO 34** | Reads light intensity   |
| **LDR**              | DO (Digital Out) | **GPIO 27** | Digital light threshold |
| **Sound Sensor**     | AO (Analog Out)  | **GPIO 35** | Reads sound intensity   |
| **Sound Sensor**     | DO (Digital Out) | **GPIO 26** | Detects sound threshold |
| **PIR Sensor**       | OUT              | **GPIO 33** | Detects motion          |
| **WS281B LED Strip** | DIN              | **GPIO 25** | Sends LED data          |

### Power / Ground

```text
LDR
├── VCC → 3.3V
├── GND → GND
├── AO  → GPIO 34
└── DO  → GPIO 27


Sound Sensor
├── VCC → 3.3V
├── GND → GND
├── AO  → GPIO 35
└── DO  → GPIO 26


PIR
├── VCC → 5V
├── GND → GND
└── OUT → GPIO 33


WS281B — 100 LEDs
├── DIN → GPIO 25
├── GND → External PSU GND
└── 5V  → External 5V PSU
```

### ⚡ Critical LED power connection

The **ESP32 and LED power supply share GND**:

```text
                 ┌── WS281B GND
                 │
External PSU GND ┼── ESP32 GND
                 │
                 └── common ground
```

The LED strip's power **does not come from the ESP32**.

```text
External 5V PSU
      │
      ├── +5V ─────────→ WS281B 5V
      │
      └── GND ─────────→ WS281B GND
                            │
ESP32 GND ──────────────────┘
```
